English

Hillguard is an offline game. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data, and it does not track you.

  • No internet: the game does not connect to the internet to play. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics.
  • Your progress stays on your device: gold, stars, upgrades and settings are saved only on your phone or tablet. Your device's own backup (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include them under your account with Apple or Google. Deleting the game deletes them.
  • Children: the game collects no information from anyone, including children.
  • Changes: if a future version adds anything that uses data (for example ads), this page will say so before that version is released, and the game will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
